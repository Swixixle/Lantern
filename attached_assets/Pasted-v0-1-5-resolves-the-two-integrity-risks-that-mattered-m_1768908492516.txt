The one thing I would tighten before calling it “done”

Your canonicalization must be truly canonical, or you will get phantom pack_id changes.

Make sure the canonical pack JSON:
	•	sorts arrays deterministically (especially items)
	•	sorts object keys deterministically
	•	normalizes whitespace and quote characters only where you already do so in extraction (do not invent new normalization during hashing)
	•	excludes volatile fields (timestamps, UI-only fields, debug counters)

If any of those are not true, pack IDs will “change for no reason,” and your library will accumulate noise.

A simple rule:
	•	Hash only the fields you would be comfortable treating as “the artifact.”
