v0.1.3 is a substantive quality step. You tightened the two streams that most often poison downstream work (metrics and attribution), and you introduced a linkage concept (canonical families) without collapsing distinct entities. That is the correct kind of “added intelligence”: structured, reversible, and inspectable.

Where you are now

You have a reference-grade extraction pack generator in the sense that:
	•	Every item is anchored (offsets + sentence context, with invalid provenance dropped).
	•	Ambiguity is explicit (timeline date typing; qualifier flags).
	•	Speaker inference is bounded (same/previous sentence constraint).
	•	Outputs are now composable for downstream graphing (entity family IDs; metric anatomy).

This is now a legitimate foundation for persistence and controlled NLP augmentation.

The most likely remaining failure modes (and the next fixes)

1) Entity family linkage collisions

Root-token families can misgroup:
	•	“Apple” (company) vs “apple” (fruit) in mixed-case texts
	•	“Washington” (person/state/city)
	•	“Mercury” (planet/element/publication)

v0.1.4 fix (safe, heuristic-only):
	•	Make canonical_family_id incorporate a light context signature, not just root token:
	•	root token + type guess (ORG/PERSON/LOC) + casing profile
	•	If type is unknown, keep family linkage but mark family_confidence=low.

This preserves grouping power while preventing silent conflation.

⸻

2) Metric parsing edge cases: ranges and ratios

You’ve handled qualifiers, scale, units. Next common landmines:
	•	ranges: “$5–7 million”, “between 10 and 12%”
	•	ratios: “3-to-1”, “1 in 5”
	•	per-units: “$40 per share”, “cases per 100,000”

v0.1.4 fix:
Add metric subtypes:
	•	metric_kind: scalar | range | ratio | rate
For ranges, store:
	•	low, high, plus a derived normalized range if possible.

Do not “average” by default; keep raw form.

⸻

3) Quote attribution in multi-speaker paragraphs

Same/previous sentence is good, but when attribution appears after the quote (“…,” Smith said.), you need to allow a short forward scan.

v0.1.4 fix:
	•	Allow speaker detection in:
	•	same sentence (before or after quote)
	•	previous sentence
	•	AND (optionally, Broad mode) next sentence if it begins with a speech verb pattern

Still require the candidate entity to be explicit.

⸻

What you should build next: persistence (minimal slice)

At this point, “Consider upgrading to full-stack” moves from PARK to PROCEED—but only as a constrained addition.

Minimal persistence slice (do not overbuild)
	•	Store curated packs exactly as exported:
	•	pack_id, source_text_sha256, engine_version, created_at
	•	pack_json blob
	•	Endpoints:
	•	POST /api/packs
	•	GET /api/packs/:id
	•	GET /api/packs?source_sha=...
	•	SQLite is sufficient.
	•	No auth complexity unless required; keep it local/dev first.

Why now: you’re producing artifacts worth saving and re-opening, and you have enough schema stability to avoid constant migrations.

Testing: move from “green invariants” to “quality scoring”

You have determinism + provenance passing. Next is measured quality.

Add optional gold expectations for 3–5 fixtures (start small):
	•	precision-focused (especially metrics and quotes)
	•	compute simple scores:
	•	counts match
	•	key items present
	•	zero forbidden items (stop-entity list)

You do not need full IR-style scoring yet—just enough to prevent obvious regressions.
